My Approach

Business-First AI Thinking

I believe the best AI solutions start with understanding the business, not the technology. Before writing a single prompt or designing an agent, I ask: who owns this process? Where does the data live? What does “better” actually mean for this team?

Philosophy
Outcomes over outputs
Method
Discovery → Design → Validate
Stakeholders
IT Ops, Support Teams, Leadership
Success Metric
ROI Created, Time Saved, Errors Reduced

How I Approach Projects

  • Map the current workflow before proposing automation
  • Identify the 20% of work causing 80% of the pain
  • Start small with high-impact, low-risk use cases
  • Measure baseline metrics before and after implementation
